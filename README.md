# Ex.No-11-Creating-a-Simple-Professional-Profile-Page-on-LinkedIn

# AIM

To understand the importance of a professional online presence and to create a simple, complete, and professional profile page on LinkedIn, highlighting academic and professional details in a clear and organised manner.

# WHAT IS LINKEDIN?

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform used by students, faculty members, and working professionals to showcase their education, skills, experience, and achievements. It also helps users connect with professionals, discover internships and job opportunities, and stay updated with industry developments.

A well-designed LinkedIn profile can act as an online professional résumé and should contain accurate, complete, and clearly presented information.

# TOOLS REQUIRED

Web browser such as Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
LinkedIn mobile application (optional)
Valid email ID or phone number
Recent and clear profile photograph
Educational and professional details
List of relevant skills
Background or cover image (optional)

# PROCEDURE

Step 1: Preparing Your Information

Before creating the profile, prepare the required information in a document. The details may include:

Full name
Current designation or student status
Institution name
Location
Short About summary
Educational qualifications
Work or project experience
Technical and professional skills

Preparing the information in advance makes profile creation easier and avoids incomplete entries.

Step 2: Signing Up on LinkedIn
Open a web browser.
Visit the LinkedIn website.
Select Join now.
Enter a valid email address or phone number.
Create a secure password.
Enter the required personal details.
Verify the account using the confirmation method provided.
Step 3: Adding a Profile Photo and Background Image
Open the profile page.
Select the profile photo option.
Upload a clear and recent photograph.
Add a suitable background/banner image if required.
Adjust the image to fit the profile layout.

A clear profile photo and suitable banner provide a professional appearance.

Step 4: Adding Name, Headline, and Location

Select the profile editing option and enter:

Name
Professional headline
Location

Example headline:

"B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering Student | Interested in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning"

The headline should briefly describe the user's current role and professional interests.

Step 5: Writing the About Section

Add a short professional summary describing the user's academic background, interests, skills, and career goals.

Example:

I am an engineering student interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Electronics. I am developing my technical and programming skills through academic projects and practical learning. I am interested in exploring emerging technologies and applying them to real-world problems.

Step 6: Adding Education Details

Add the relevant educational information:

Institution name
Degree
Field of study
Start year
Expected completion year
Relevant activities or achievements, if applicable

Example:

Degree: B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering
Institution: Saveetha Engineering College
Status: Student

Step 7: Adding Experience Details

Add professional experience, internships, projects, or other relevant activities.

For each entry, provide:

Position/title
Organisation
Employment type
Location
Duration
Description of responsibilities

For a student without formal employment, academic projects, internships, or relevant training can be included where appropriate.

Step 8: Adding Skills

Add skills relevant to the user's academic or professional field.

Examples:

Python
Artificial Intelligence
Machine Learning
Data Analysis
Electronics
Communication
Problem Solving
Teamwork

Only skills that accurately represent the user's knowledge or experience should be added.

Step 9: Reviewing Profile and Privacy Settings

Review all profile sections to ensure that the information is:

Correct
Complete
Professional
Clearly written
Free from spelling errors

Review the account's visibility and privacy settings and choose what information should be visible to others.

Step 10: Publishing and Sharing the Profile
Open the completed profile.
Review how the profile appears to other users.
Copy the profile URL using the available sharing option.
The profile link can be included in a résumé, portfolio, email signature, or other professional documents.

# SAMPLE OUTPUT SCREEN

The completed profile page should display the following major sections:

Profile photograph
Background/banner image
Name
Professional headline
Location
About section
Education
Experience or Projects
Skills

The final profile should have a clean and professional appearance.

A simple and professional LinkedIn profile page was created successfully with a profile photo, background banner, headline, About section, Education, Experience/Projects, and Skills. The completed profile was reviewed and prepared for sharing through a profile link.

Thus, a simple and professional LinkedIn profile page was successfully created with the essential academic and professional information organised clearly.


Creating a LinkedIn profile helps students and professionals establish a professional online presence. By providing accurate information in sections such as Photo, Headline, About, Education, Experience, and Skills, users can present their academic and professional background in an organised manner and connect with the wider professional community.
